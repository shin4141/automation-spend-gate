# Example: Company SaaS Seat Unused By Team Member

## Spend Summary

- Spend: Company SaaS seat
- Amount: Per-seat monthly or annual cost
- Billing period: Monthly or annual
- Payment or renewal date: Next renewal or seat true-up
- Tool, service, person, or infrastructure: SaaS seat assigned to one team member

## Renewal Gate

Before this renews, what evidence justifies paying again?

The assigned team member has not meaningfully used the seat. Renewal requires evidence that the seat creates output, reduces failure rate, or is operationally required.

## Profit Gradient

The question is not whether the product is good. The question is whether this seat creates future profit, time saved, reusable assets, or reduced failure rate.

## No-Use Cut Rule

If there is no meaningful use, no concrete next-7-day use, low rejoin cost, and low data/operation risk, the seat should not renew by default.

## Decision

`STOP` or `RESCALE`

## Decision Rationale

Stop the unused seat if the team member does not need it. Rescale seats if the company still needs the product but fewer people need paid access.

## Rejoin Condition

Rejoin when the team member has a named workflow, deliverable, or operational responsibility that needs the paid seat.

## Protected Margin

Protected margin is the avoided seat cost from removing or reducing unused access.

## Next Safe Action

Audit seat usage before renewal and remove, downgrade, or reassign unused seats.
