# Example: Positive-EV Tool Under Budget Pressure

## Spend Summary

- Spend: AI coding or automation tool
- Amount: Current paid plan
- Billing period: Monthly or usage-based
- Payment or renewal date: Next renewal or credit purchase
- Tool, service, person, or infrastructure: Tool that is producing useful output

## Renewal Gate

Before this renews, what evidence justifies paying again?

The tool is creating useful assets, but the current plan is larger than the near-term build window can support.

## Profit Gradient

The spend has positive EV because it saves time and creates reusable assets. Budget pressure does not automatically mean the tool should be stopped.

## Decision

`RESCALE`

## Decision Rationale

Keep the option value while reducing exposure. Move to a cheaper plan, shorter paid window, lower usage cap, or batch-work pattern instead of canceling a tool that is producing results.

## Rejoin Condition

Scale back up when the next 7-day KPI requires the higher plan and the expected output is named before payment.

## Protected Margin

Protected margin is the difference between the current plan and the smaller plan during the constrained period.

## Next Safe Action

Choose the smallest plan or usage cap that can still produce the next durable asset.
