# Example: Industry Fit For A Heavy Workflow

## Spend Summary

- Spend: Highest AI plan for a company workflow
- Amount: Higher-tier plan or expanded tool bundle
- Billing period: Monthly or annual
- Payment or renewal date: Before upgrade or renewal
- Tool, service, person, or infrastructure: AI tools for an approval-heavy or verification-heavy workflow

## Situation

The company has budget and wants the highest AI plan. The workflow is approval-heavy or verification-heavy, so output cannot move straight from model response to business value.

## Industry Fit

The workflow may still benefit from AI, but model quality is not the bottleneck yet. The bottleneck is mapping the workflow, finding the review points, and proving where AI output actually saves time or reduces failure risk.

## Decision

`WAIT` or `RESCALE`

## Decision Rationale

Do not buy the highest plan just because the company can afford it. Workflow mapping and a smaller pilot are higher EV than expanding spend before the profit gradient is visible.

## Rejoin / Upgrade Condition

Upgrade only when a specific workflow shows repeated output, saved time, or reduced failure risk.

## Next Safe Action

Run a smaller pilot on one workflow, document the approval path, and measure whether AI output survives review.
