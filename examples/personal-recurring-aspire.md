# Example: Personal Recurring Cost With Aspire

## Spend Summary

- Spend: Gym, Netflix-style service, or similar recurring personal cost
- Amount: Monthly plan
- Billing period: Monthly
- Payment or renewal date: Next renewal date
- Tool, service, person, or infrastructure: Personal recurring service

## Renewal Gate

Before this renews, what evidence justifies paying again?

The service may represent a real Aspire, such as health, rest, learning, or creative recovery. The gate should preserve the Aspire without letting an unused billing cycle renew by default.

## Profit Gradient

For this edge case, profit gradient means the spend should create time saved, reduced failure rate, durable wellbeing, or a protected routine. It is still not generic lifestyle advice.

## No-Use Cut Rule

If there is no meaningful use in the last N days, no concrete next-7-day use, low rejoin cost, and low data/operation risk, stop the recurring charge.

## Decision

`STOP`

## Decision Rationale

Stop the recurring payment while preserving the underlying Aspire. The Aspire may still matter, but the current subscription is not the active path.

## Rejoin Condition

Rejoin when there is a concrete next-7-day plan, such as scheduled sessions, a specific show/project, or a routine that will actually use the service.

## Protected Margin

Protected margin is the avoided recurring spend while the Aspire is pursued through a cheaper, smaller, or manual path.

## Next Safe Action

Cancel or pause before renewal, then write the smallest next action that still honors the Aspire.
