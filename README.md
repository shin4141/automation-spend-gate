# Automation Spend Gate

Every AI tool looks worth it. Together they can kill your Rescale options.

"Rescale options" means keeping cheaper, smaller, reversible paths open until the project proves it deserves more cost.

Automation Spend Gate is a pre-payment EV gate for AI automation costs. It asks one practical question:

> Before you pay, what must be true in 7 days for this spend to be justified?

Use it before paying for tools, APIs, SaaS, model credits, hosting, ads, freelancers, or automation infrastructure.

## Who It Is For

Automation Spend Gate is for AI builders, vibe coders, and automation operators who need to decide whether a spend creates a real next option or just another recurring drag.

## What It Forces

Before spending, define:

- what the spend should unlock
- what durable asset it should create
- what KPI must be reached
- when to cancel or stop
- what cheaper or reversible option exists
- what happens if you wait

## Decision Labels

- `GO`: Pay now, with a specific 7-day KPI and stop-loss.
- `WAIT`: Do not pay yet. Use the current free, paid, or manual window first.
- `RESCALE`: Choose a cheaper, smaller, reversible, or shorter option.
- `STOP`: Do not continue. The spend is not creating enough option value.

## Boundaries

This is not:

- personal finance advice
- tax advice
- investment advice
- subscription tracker
- budgeting app
- profit guarantee

It is:

- pre-payment decision gate
- stop-loss prompt
- Rescale protection checklist
- way to convert "this tool seems useful" into a time-boxed EV decision
